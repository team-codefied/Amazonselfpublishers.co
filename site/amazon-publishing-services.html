Overview

What this service covers

Amazon offers independent authors a direct route to readers, but the set-up has many moving parts: trim sizes, bleed, file types, metadata, categories, pricing and rights choices. Mistakes at this stage can delay your release or make the finished book look unprofessional.

We prepare the files, guide you through the account and listing decisions, and make sure what you upload matches the platform requirements.

Who it is for

  • Authors ready to upload a finished manuscript
  • Writers unsure about formats, trim sizes or categories
  • Authors whose files were rejected or look wrong in preview
  • Anyone planning ebook and paperback editions together

What you receive

Deliverables and scope

  1. 01

    Plain explanations of the choices you make on the platform, so your own account stays under your control.

  2. 02

    Ebook and print file preparation

    Interior and cover files built to the specification for each edition you choose.

  3. 03

    Title and subtitle review

    A check that your title data is consistent across cover, listing and files.

  4. 04

    Categories and keywords

    Research-led suggestions for categories and search phrases that match what your book is about.

  5. 05

    A readable, honest book description written to help the right reader decide to buy.

  6. 06

    Pre-publication checklist

    A final walkthrough of previews, proofs, pricing and release timing.

FAQ

Amazon Publishing Support: frequently asked questions

Can I publish on Amazon myself?

Yes. Amazon KDP is free to use and you can publish there directly at kdp.amazon.com. We are an independent company, not affiliated with Amazon, and we charge only for the editing, design, formatting and publishing support you choose.

Are you affiliated with Amazon?

No. We are an independent publishing-services company. We are not owned by, endorsed by or officially partnered with Amazon.

Will I publish from my own account?

Yes. We guide you through the process so the account, royalties and book remain yours.

Can you help if my files were rejected?

Usually, yes. Send us the notice you received and the files, and we will tell you what needs to change.

Do you guarantee sales or rankings?

No. Sales depend on many factors, including the book, genre, pricing and promotion. We make sure your release is professional and complete.

Can I publish both ebook and paperback?

Yes. We can prepare both editions so the covers, text and listing stay consistent.
