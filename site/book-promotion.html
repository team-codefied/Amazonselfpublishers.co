Overview

What this service covers

Promotion has to match the book. A cosy mystery calls for different channels than a business handbook. We pick places where your readers already spend time.

We handle the groundwork, which is often the slow part, and report back plainly on what happened.

FAQ

Book Promotion: frequently asked questions

Do you buy reviews?

No. Paid or fake reviews break platform rules. We only pursue genuine reader feedback.

What results can I expect?

We cannot promise specific sales or rankings. We report what we did and what happened.

How long does a campaign run?

Typically several weeks around a launch, with options to continue.

Is promotion different from marketing?

Marketing is the overall strategy, and promotion is the activity that carries it out.
