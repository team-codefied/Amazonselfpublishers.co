FAQ

Book Publishing: Frequently Asked Questions

Can I publish on Amazon myself?

Yes. Amazon KDP is free to use and you can publish there directly at kdp.amazon.com. We are an independent company, not affiliated with Amazon, and we charge only for the editing, design, formatting and publishing support you choose.

Can I select only some of your services?

Yes. You can choose the services that suit your project. We can discuss your requirements and explain the available options before you decide.

How long does book publishing take?

The timeline depends on your manuscript, the services selected and the work required. We can discuss an estimated timeline after reviewing your project.

Do you offer manuscript editing?

Editing and proofreading support can be discussed according to your manuscript's condition and requirements.

How do I get started?

Complete the quote form with your contact details and information about your book. Our team can then discuss your requirements and the next steps.

Do I keep the rights to my book?

Authors retain ownership of their work. Any agreement should state this plainly, and we are happy to discuss it before you commit to anything.

Do you promise bestseller status?

No one can honestly promise that. We focus on producing a professional book and giving it the best possible start.
